### Task 1 — GlowNest Viability Analysis
I worked through all six questions for the fictional skincare brand GlowNest:
- Calculated unit economics: gross margin ₹520, Amazon fee ₹96, ad cost ₹200, net margin ₹224 per ad unit
- Monthly net profit came out to ₹10,34,000 on ₹28L revenue
- ACOS is 25% — healthy, well below the 53% break-even ceiling
- TACOS is ~10.7% — strong, because organic sales dilute the ad spend weight
- Break-even ad spend is ₹13.34L/month — GlowNest has huge headroom vs its current ₹3L spend
- Ran both scenarios (ACOS worsening to 40%, and doubling ad spend to ₹6L)

### Task 2 — Dynamic Profitability Model
Built a multi-sheet Excel model with 113 formulas and zero errors. All inputs are in editable blue cells — change any one and every output recalculates automatically. Included four scenario columns side by side: price increase to ₹950, COGS inflation +20%, organic growth to 3,500 units, and competitor price pressure dropping SP to ₹700. Conditional formatting flags losses in red and TACOS above 15% in yellow.

### Task 3A — Weekly Status Update (V1 Work, V3 Eyes)
Wrote the status update for all three brands but went beyond the numbers. I flagged three patterns:
1. Brand A and Brand C asked the exact same question about the report columns in the same week — that is a report design problem, not a client education problem
2. Brand B consumed 12 hours of work but the client thinks nothing is happening — the effort is invisible and that is a churn risk
3. Brand C has an active Flipkart store with zero advertising — nobody asked me to notice this but it is either a cross-sell opportunity or a question worth raising

### Task 3B — MD Talking Points
I picked UrbanPulse as the most interesting account. Prepared talking points for a 15-minute call with the MD — framed entirely in business language (revenue trajectory, competitive gaps, growth levers) not campaign metrics. The central insight: the listing image quality is a conversion rate problem, not an ad problem, and fixing it is the highest-ROI action available at zero additional ad spend.
